# Tenant configuration template

This is a template only. Copy it into a tenant-specific, access-controlled configuration store during provisioning.

- Legal business name: REQUIRED
- Legal entity and tax identifiers: REQUIRED through secure onboarding; never store secrets in agent files
- Jurisdictions of employment: REQUIRED
- Time zone: REQUIRED
- Payroll frequency and workweek definition: REQUIRED
- Approved employee handbook version: REQUIRED
- Authorized administrators and approvers: REQUIRED
- Communication number and consent configuration: REQUIRED
- Payroll provider and tax filing configuration: REQUIRED before live payroll

Do not activate production workflows while required configuration is missing or unverified.
